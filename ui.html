Player Trading: Counter-Offer Negotiation

A clean Roblox trading interface designed for player item exchanges and value-based negotiations. Features side-by-side OLD vs NEW counter offer comparison, live RAP and Value metrics, and modular 4-slot item grids.

  • Dual-tier comparison: Side-by-side OLD vs NEW offer comparison allowing players to clearly review counter-offer changes.
  • Live Value & RAP metrics: Real-time valuation headers on both sides of the trade to ensure fair transactions.
  • Modular 4-slot containers: Touch-friendly item slots with subtle transparency, rounded corners, and crisp asset framing.
  • Negotiation workflow: Structured layout for high-stakes simulator economies with intuitive accept and counter actions.

Advantage

Why Hire a UI Designer Who Scripts?

Most Roblox designers only hand over flat image files. Having four years of Luau development experience changes how I build interfaces.

01

Responsive Cross-Platform

Every element is designed with proper AnchorPoints, Scale sizing, and UIAspectRatioConstraints so your UI never squashes or clips on phones, tablets, PCs, or consoles.

02

TweenService Ready

Button click squashes, modal spring pop-ins, hover lighting, and CanvasGroup transparency transitions are anticipated in the layout hierarchy from day one.

03

Zero Hand-off Friction

No more back-and-forth between a designer and a programmer arguing over broken slices or misaligned frames. I can deliver the graphics or script the complete UI functionality directly.
