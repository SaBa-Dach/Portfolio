Concept 01 · Simulator Interface

Pet / Item Inventory System

A cartoon simulator inventory modal built for high-retention pet collecting experiences. Features bold comic typography, bright tactile action buttons, and clear item organization.

  • Header banner: Cyan textured background with drop-shadow 3D comic typography (INVENTORY!) and bold red exit button (X).
  • Toolbar: Integrated search bar with placeholder styling, plus quick action pills (Equip Best, Delete Mode, and Filter).
  • Dynamic grid slots: 10-slot responsive item grid displaying pet icons, customizable item names, and Level 999 stats badges.
  • Rarity gradients: Tiered background cards (Legendary Gold & Mythic Green) with comic halftone dot texture overlays.

Concept 02 · Store & HUD Design

In-Game Shop & Navigation HUD

A full in-game monetization store window coupled with persistent HUD navigation and currency display, styled with classic Roblox stud textures and vibrant pop-art borders.

  • Top Category Bar: Roblox stud-textured category tabs (Sell, Base, and active Shop) with bold outlines.

Advantage

Why Hire a UI Designer Who Scripts?

Most Roblox designers only hand over flat image files. Having four years of Luau development experience changes how I build interfaces.

01

Responsive Cross-Platform

Every element is designed with proper AnchorPoints, Scale sizing, and UIAspectRatioConstraints so your UI never squashes or clips on phones, tablets, PCs, or consoles.

02

TweenService Ready

Button click squashes, modal spring pop-ins, hover lighting, and CanvasGroup transparency transitions are anticipated in the layout hierarchy from day one.

03

Zero Hand-off Friction

No more back-and-forth between a designer and a programmer arguing over broken slices or misaligned frames. I can deliver the graphics or script the complete UI functionality directly.
